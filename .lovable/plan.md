# Re-render SKETCH_2 as one U-shaped, outward-facing bookshelf

## What the two drawings actually show
Both PDFs show the same freestanding U-shaped bookshelf, with every shelf opening facing outward. Each drawing is taken from outside the U:

- **SKETCH_2_FRONT** is a straight-on view of the long side of the U from outside. It has 3 tiers. Closed modules, each with a back panel, alternate with open bays that have no back, so you see through them.
- **SKETCH_2_SIDE** is a 3/4 view from outside, looking at one short leg of the U:
  - **Short leg (front of the drawing):** 4 columns of cubbies, 3 tiers, opening toward the viewer. Modules have backs; the open bay does not.
  - **Long side (going back on the right):** its cubbies open outward to the right, so you see shelf edges and the top board. It is the same long side as in the FRONT drawing.
  - **Far leg (top left):** seen from behind. Only the modules show back panels; the bays between them are open.

## What went wrong in the previous renders
- **3/4 render:** the long side came out as a solid closed wall with panels that look like drawers. It should have open, outward-facing cubbies that match the FRONT drawing.
- **Front render:** it shows a bookshelf pushed against a wall. It should be the outside face of a freestanding U. Some of its open bays and modules are also in the wrong places.
- **Both:** the two photos were not made as views of the same piece of furniture.

## Plan
1. Use the FRONT drawing as the reference for the long side: the same sequence of modules and open bays, 3 tiers.
2. Remake the front view as the long outside face of a freestanding U. Show a slight hint that the two legs turn back at each end. Only the modules have backs. Through the open bays you see the room, not a wall right behind them.
3. Remake the 3/4 view from scratch, using both drawings together as references:
   - the short leg's 4 columns facing the camera
   - the long side going back with open cubbies facing outward, matching step 1
   - the far leg seen from behind, with backs only on the modules
4. Keep all the existing rules:
   - natural maple
   - sitting on the floor with a contact shadow, no feet
   - modern home with a 2.7 m ceiling and soft side light from a window
   - catalogue look, with no people, books, objects or text
   - long boards split at the 70" limit
5. Use the same room, light and wood in both photos so they clearly show one piece.
6. Check each photo against its drawing before delivering: tiers, column order, where the backs are, and which way the openings face. Redo any photo that doesn't match.
7. Save the new photos as new versions (`sketch2-front-v2.jpg`, `sketch2-side-3-4-v4.jpg`), without replacing the old ones.

No app code changes.
